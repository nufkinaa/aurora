The real App.tsx and src/, bundled for the browser. ←↑↓→ move focus, Enter is OK, Esc is Back, P play/pause, F/R fast-forward/rewind. Click a frame to drive just that one when mirroring is off. Sizes are in dp, the unit the app lays out in: the Google TV Streamer and most 1080p sets are 960×540, many sets 1280×720, some 4K sets 1920×1080.
