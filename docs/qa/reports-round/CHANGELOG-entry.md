## 1.10.0 — 2026-10-2x

**The reports round: a bad copy can be swapped, cinema copies stay out, AirPlay on iPhone.**

- **Get another version.** A copy in the library with burned-in subtitles, a bad picture, bad sound or the wrong language can be replaced by anyone grown-up, without the admin: Problem with this copy? on the title page (to the right of Versions on the TV), an episode's sources (hold it) or the player's menu. Say what is wrong, pick another version (★ BEST first, the copy you have left out) and it downloads like any of your downloads; the copy you have plays until the new one is in, then it takes its place under the same library entry, so your place, My List, ratings and links stay. If someone is watching the old copy, the swap waits until they stop. Cancel at any point, or a failed download, leaves everything as it was. Kids profiles don't see it. The release that was reported is remembered: nothing automatic picks it again and it is never ★ BEST. The admin sees every replacement in the Inbox (Replaced copies), in Problem reports with the reason, on the person's sheet and on the download row.
- **Cinema copies are never picked for you.** Aurora reads every release name for copies made before a film is out at home — CAM, TS / telesync, TC / telecine, screeners and early "HC" copies — and for subtitles burned into the picture. Those copies are listed last, marked (CAM, TS, BURNED-IN SUBS, with the reason in words), and asked about first ("This is not the real release — Download it anyway / Play it anyway"); burned-in copies come after every clean one. ★ BEST, Save & watch, the TV's one-press Play, episode downloads, smart downloads, follows and the second-source race never take a pre-release copy. A film that only has cinema copies says "Not out in good quality yet — only cinema recordings so far" (or "only early copies so far", or "Not out to watch at home yet") with its release dates, where Save & watch was; on the TV under the button, which then reads ≡ Sources. The same marks and question are in Get another version, and replacing a copy because of burned-in subtitles never stars another copy with burned-in subtitles.
- **My List only adds.** Adding a title to My List no longer starts a download (the toast just says "Added to My List"); My downloads and the TV lose the MY LIST / On hold rows and Start now. Copies My List already fetched keep their tidy-up (stale after 14 days, removed after 21, never one you downloaded by hand, watched or the admin kept), and its queued downloads carry on as ordinary automatic downloads. The admin card reads "Left from My List downloads" until nothing is left. Downloads: people's own first, then automatic, three per account.
- **AirPlay from an iPhone.** Sending a film from an iPhone to an Apple TV on a server with sign-in no longer plays only the sound: the iPhone now hands the TV an address it can open as soon as an Apple TV is in reach. With subtitles on, the TV gets a full description of the picture; "Playing on the TV · No picture there?" switches the TV to a copy any Apple TV plays, and a TV that refuses steps down one thing at a time (subtitles, a fresh link, a plain H.264 copy) before saying so. Stopping AirPlay resumes on the phone where the TV was.
- **No more freezes and restarts on an iPhone.** On a slow line the iPhone's own player no longer drops to a 480p/720p re-encode because of a rule meant for other browsers; a hiccup is retried in place (up to three times) instead of starting a full re-encode from 0:00; a stall recovers by itself (a nudge at 20 s, a reload at 45 s, a message at 90 s); none of this happens while AirPlay is on.
- **Aurora TV 5.5.0:** Get another version (title page, an episode's hold menu, the player's Help menu), cinema-copy marks and the "This is not the real release" question on Sources and in Get another version, "Not out in good quality yet" on the title page, My List only adds; ★ BEST and one-press Play never take a pre-release copy or a release reported bad here.
