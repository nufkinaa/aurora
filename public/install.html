Install it on the TV with Downloader

  1. Get Downloader. On the TV, open the Play Store, search for Downloader (by AFTVnews) and install it.
  2. Let it install apps. Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources → turn on Downloader. On some Google TV models this switch only shows once developer mode is on: Settings → System → About → press Android TV OS build seven times.
  3. Type the address. Open Downloader, type {{host}}/download and press Go. When it has downloaded, press Install, then Open.
  4. Sign in. The TV shows a code — scan it with your phone, or type it at {{host}}/link.

Scan with a phone to open the download link — handy for sending it to the TV.

Features

Everything a streaming service does. On a server that is yours.

A Home that knows you

Continue watching right where you stopped, recommendations that learn what you like, everything new on the server — and a big, beautiful billboard to start the evening.

  • One profile per person, kids profiles with age limits
  • My List, ratings and "More like this"
  • Search by voice, in more than one language

Made for the big screen

The Android TV app is built for the remote — fast, smooth on modest TV boxes, and with the same profiles, lists and progress as everywhere else.

  • Sign in by scanning a code with your phone
  • Plays the file directly when the TV can, converts when it can't
  • Updates itself when a new version is out

Every title, properly

Poster, synopsis, cast and your own rating. Play it here, send it to the TV, or save it for the plane.

Subtitles that just work

Found in your language and matched to your copy — and if they drift, Resync listens to the film and puts them back in time.

Watch together, apart

Start a party, share the four-letter code, and everyone's play, pause and jump follow — on TVs, phones and computers alike.

Skip intro, recap and credits

Aurora hears each show's theme and offers Skip intro the moment it starts. Up next comes with the credits, not before.

Your phone is the remote

Press Play on TV on any title. Then pause, skip, change subtitles or swipe the touchpad — from the sofa, without hunting for the remote.

Recommended by your household

Loved something? Recommend it to the people you live with, with a note. It waits for them on their Home — kids only get what suits their age.
